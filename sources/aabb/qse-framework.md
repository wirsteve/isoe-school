# AABB Quality System Essentials (QSEs) — structural note, not standards text

This file records the *taxonomy* (names and themes of the ten QSEs) as a publicly
describable organizing structure — never AABB's copyrighted standards language itself.
No standard numbers, no "shall" requirement text, no glossary definitions are reproduced
here. Key-concept lines below are this course's own paraphrase, not quoted from AABB.

## What was verified, and how

1. **AABB's own live "Quality Systems Essentials" and "Updated Quality Systems
   Essentials" pages** (fetched 2026-09-22, HTTP 200):
   - https://www.aabb.org/standards-accreditation/quality/quality-systems-essentials
   - https://www.aabb.org/standards-accreditation/standards/about-aabb-standards/updated-quality-systems-essentials

   Both confirm: the original QSEs launched in **1997** (Association Bulletin #97-04)
   with **10 elements**, and AABB developed an **updated Quality Systems Essentials
   template in 2023**, which "all AABB Standards are now based on." Neither page
   enumerates the ten titles.

2. **PROPOSED Quality Systems Framework, dated September 21, 2021** (posted for public
   comment; PDF fetched via curl, HTTP 200, then text-extracted locally with `pypdf` —
   WebFetch's own PDF renderer choked on the binary stream, so extraction was done
   directly against the downloaded file):
   `https://www.aabb.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/standards/aabb-quality-systems-framework-2021.pdf?sfvrsn=a11081d1_2`

   This document spells out ten "QSE" sections, numbered QSE 1 through QSE 10, each with
   a short "Key Concepts" paragraph, key terms, and objective-evidence examples.

3. **PROPOSED 34th edition of AABB *Standards for Blood Banks and Transfusion
   Services*, posted for comment June 16 – August 15, 2023, with a stated effective date
   of April 1, 2024** (PDF fetched via curl, HTTP 200, text-extracted with `pypdf`):
   `https://www.aabb.org/docs/default-source/default-document-library/standards/proposed-34th-edition-of-standards-for-blood-banks-and-transfusion-services.pdf?sfvrsn=8d0d4f7b_0`

   This is the strongest evidence found this session: it is a dated, edition-specific
   document (not a framework discussion draft) whose own introduction states it
   "incorporated the updated quality system essentials (QSE) template for this edition"
   and that the committee chose to **"preserve chapter headings and overall structure"**
   from the QSE template. Its ten numbered chapters (Chapter 1 – Organization through
   Chapter 10 – Facilities and Safety) match the 2021 proposed framework's ten QSEs
   theme-for-theme, confirming the ten-QSE structure carried through into a specific,
   dated edition with a real effective date (April 1, 2024) that precedes today's date.
   This edition supersedes the 2021 discussion draft as the better evidence of what is
   *actually in force*, though it too was a "proposed" (comment-period) copy — AABB's
   own site does not post a plain "final, current edition, QSE list" page, so exact
   current wording of each chapter title should still be re-confirmed against whatever
   edition is on the shelf before it's cited precisely in a real Lakeshore document.

## The ten QSEs (names and one-line paraphrase, in original AABB QSE numbering)

Titles below are the clearest, most stable wording across the two documents; a couple of
titles shifted slightly between the 2021 discussion draft and the 2023/34th-edition
proposal (noted), which is itself useful teaching material — the *themes* are stable,
exact wording drifts by edition.

1. **Organization** — executive management's responsibility and authority for the
   quality system, management review, and who can approve exceptions to policy.
2. **Resources** — staffing, job qualifications, competence assessment, training.
3. **Equipment** — equipment selection, qualification (including IQ/OQ/PQ-style
   language), monitoring, and maintenance — explicitly includes IT systems.
4. **Supplier and Customer Issues** — titled "Supplier and Customer Agreements" in the
   2021 draft and "Suppliers and Customers" in the 2023/34th-edition proposal — qualifying
   and managing suppliers and customer agreements.
5. **Process Control** — the largest QSE by page count in both documents: validation of
   processes, labeling, and (per the 34th-edition proposal) the operational content of
   collection, testing, and manufacturing steps.
6. **Documents and Records** — document lifecycle and record retention; this QSE ends
   with the comprehensive record-retention table in both documents.
7. **Deviations, Nonconformances, and Adverse Events** — capture, assessment,
   investigation, and disposition of events that don't meet requirements, and reporting
   to outside agencies. The 34th-edition proposal cites **21 CFR 606.171** and **21 CFR
   1271.350** directly in this chapter's introductory standard (606.171 is already used
   elsewhere in this curriculum as the blood biological-product-deviation-report trigger).
8. **Assessments: Internal and External** — internal quality assessment plus support for
   external assessments by accreditors, health authorities, and regulators.
9. **Process Improvement** — titled "Process Improvement Through Corrective and
   Preventive Action" in an older template, per AABB's own site language.
10. **Facilities and Safety** — safety and adequacy of physical space, biohazard
    disposal, environmental monitoring.

## What is still NOT verified — do not overclaim beyond this

- The **exact chapter-title wording currently in force** on any given AABB Standards
  set (blood bank/transfusion service vs. cellular therapy vs. relationship testing) —
  titles drift slightly edition to edition, as shown above for QSE 4.
- Any **standard number** inside a QSE (e.g., a specific numbered standard for change
  control or management review) — both fetched PDFs are proposed/comment-period drafts;
  numbering in a comment draft is not guaranteed to match the adopted final text. Do not
  cite a standard number from either PDF as a current, adopted AABB standard number.
- Whether a **35th edition or later** has since superseded the 34th edition described
  here.

## Do not reproduce

Do not copy any "shall" requirement sentence, "Key Concepts" paragraph, glossary
definition, or "Examples of Objective Evidence" bullet list verbatim from either PDF into
a lesson. AABB Standards text is copyrighted. Only the *names and themes* above (this
course's own paraphrase) are used in the lesson.
