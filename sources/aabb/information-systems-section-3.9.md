## Section 3.9 structure (numbers and themes only — paraphrase, not AABB's text)

Numbering below reflects the structure cross-confirmed across the Fundamental Standards
and recent Standards for Blood Banks and Transfusion Services editions. Edition-to-edition
numbering can shift (the 35th-edition proposal was reported, in secondary sources, to be
reorganizing this content into a broader quality-systems chapter) — treat the *themes* as
stable and the *exact sub-number* as edition-dependent, the same caveat `qse-framework.md`
already carries for QSE titles.

- **3.9 (overall)** — processes covering the implementation and modification of
  information system software, hardware, and databases: risk analysis, validation,
  training, implementation, post-implementation evaluation, ongoing maintenance and
  operation.
- **3.9.1 — Records.** Records of system validation, lifecycle compliance, version
  designation/numbering, user-defined tables, electronic data transfer/receipt, and
  monitoring of critical data-element integrity.
- **3.9.2 — Backup system / continuity.** A manual or computer-based alternate system
  maintained ready for continuous operation if the computerized system fails, tested at
  defined intervals, supported by disaster mitigation and recovery plans.
- **3.9.3 — Personnel.** Those who manage information systems are responsible for
  regulatory compliance in how the systems are used.
- **3.9.4 — Documented processes.** A documented process for managing information
  systems, covering implementation, use, ongoing support, and modification.
- **3.9.5 — Access control.** A system to prevent unauthorized access to computers and
  electronic records, established and followed.
