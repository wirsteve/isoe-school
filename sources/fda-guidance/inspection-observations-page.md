# FDA public page — Inspection Observations (Form FDA 483)

Source: U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Office of Inspections and Investigations (OII) public
web page, "Inspection Observations."
URL: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/inspection-references/inspection-observations
Retrieved: 2026-10-09 (fda.gov reachable this session; HTTP 200).
Tier: FDA public informational page (not a guidance document, not regulation) — describes FDA's
own administrative practice for issuing Form FDA 483.

Extracted text (relevant portion, HTML stripped):

---

FDA's Office of Inspections and Investigations (OII) is the lead office for all field activities,
including inspections and enforcement. During an inspection, OII investigators may observe
conditions they deem to be objectionable. These observations are listed on an FDA Form 483 when,
in an investigator's judgment, the observed conditions or practices indicate that an
FDA-regulated product may be in violation of FDA's requirements.

Spreadsheets summarizing the areas of regulation cited on FDA's system-generated 483s are
available by fiscal year... These spreadsheets are not a comprehensive listing of all
inspectional observations but represent the area of regulation and the number of times it was
cited as an observation on a Form FDA 483 during inspections conducted by FDA and its
representatives. Inspectional observations reflect data pulled from FDA's electronic inspection
tools. These tools are used to generate the Form FDA 483 when necessary. Not all Form FDA 483s
are generated by these tools as some 483s are manually prepared.

---

Note for drafters: this page confirms Form FDA 483 is FDA's own administrative instrument for
recording inspection observations — not itself defined in a specific CFR section. Its statutory
basis is 21 U.S.C. 374(b)(1) (the written inspection report requirement, FD&C Act § 704(b)) —
see `sources/usc/21-usc-374.md`. This page is silent on post-inspection classification (NAI/VAI/
OAI) — see `sources/fda-guidance/cder-gcp-inspections-outcomes-2022-excerpt.md` for that, with
its own scope caveat.
